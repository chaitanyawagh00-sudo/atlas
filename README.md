# Atlas

> An open-source Engineering Intelligence Platform.

## Vision

Atlas aims to become an Engineering Intelligence Platform that combines deterministic engineering calculations, engineering standards, and explainable AI to assist engineers in making traceable, auditable, and standards-compliant engineering decisions.

Rather than replacing engineers, Atlas is designed to augment engineering reasoning by combining:

- Deterministic engineering calculations
- International engineering standards
- Engineering knowledge
- Explainable AI
- Transparent decision making

## Current Status

🚧 Atlas is currently in the architecture and research phase.

The project is focused on designing the core platform before implementation begins.
