# Atlas Vision

## Mission

Build the world's first Engineering Intelligence Platform that combines deterministic engineering calculations, engineering standards, and explainable AI to assist engineers in making accurate, traceable, and auditable engineering decisions.

## Why Atlas Exists

Today's engineering software is fragmented.

- CAD systems create geometry.
- CAE systems perform simulations.
- PLM systems manage data.
- Engineers still perform reasoning manually using standards, spreadsheets, experience, and disconnected tools.

Atlas aims to bridge this gap by providing an engineering reasoning layer that sits above existing engineering software.

## Core Principles

1. Engineering calculations are deterministic.
2. AI assists reasoning but never invents engineering facts.
3. Every recommendation must be explainable.
4. Every engineering decision must be traceable.
5. Engineering standards are executable knowledge.
6. Human engineers always remain in control.

## Long-Term Vision

Atlas will evolve into a modular Engineering Intelligence Platform where every capability—such as bolted joints, bearings, shafts, tolerances, and design reviews—shares a common reasoning engine and architecture.
